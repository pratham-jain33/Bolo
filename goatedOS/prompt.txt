### Full feature list

**Dictation Mode**

- System-wide dictation in any standard text field
- Automatic filler-word removal, punctuation, and grammar fixes
- Context-aware formatting per app (email vs chat vs notes)
- Three polish styles: Raw, Light, or Polished
- Custom dictionary ("Spelling") with AI-assisted bulk import of names and terms\
- Hands-free dictation: double-tap your dictation key and tap once more to end it. You can also pick your own keys, and mouse buttons work as triggers
- Hallucination filtering, so it strips known false transcript phrases
- Recovers from microphone glitches and keeps the mic warm between recordings, so your first words aren't clipped
- Lowers other audio (ducking) while you speak instead of muting it

**Edit Mode**

- Select text and describe the change you want (shorten, change tone, make a list)

**Agent Mode**

- Screen-aware questions: ask about whatever is on your screen
- Web search, with answers that can include image previews, maps, stocks, charts, and YouTube cards
- Draw or circle on screen with your cursor while talking to show it what you mean
- Attach or paste images and files, and drag things into the notch
- Confirmation cards before any action that sends or changes something
- Spoken replies with adjustable voice speed and a true mute
- Long tasks run in the background and report back when done
- Natural-language reminders and scheduled or recurring actions
- Double-tap the Agent key to keep talking hands-free
- Conversation history with follow-ups and memory (including lockable core preferences)

**Integrations (App Store)**

- Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs, Google Drive, Outlook (mail and calendar),
- Claude Code and Codex integrations (beta)
- Bring your own tools through MCP servers or custom integrations, and describe an app to have VoiceOS build it in Studio (shareable by link)

**Other**

- Insights tab (speaking speed, time saved, words dictated) and a shareable "Voice Type" card
- Appearance settings (light, dark, system)

### Animations and notch style

**Notch style.** The changelog describes two notch surfaces:

- **Top notch:** where Agent Mode lives. It expands to show your transcript immediately, streams answers in, and stays open until you click away. You can also hide it.
- **Side notch:** a "liquid-glass" history view with click-to-open conversations, selected-session highlighting, and haptics. It's also where long-running background tasks show live activity, and it can be hidden when minimized.
- Appearance settings offer **live previews for supported notch materials**, though I couldn't find the specific material names.
- There's also a **"Show floating bar always"** option for when you want an indicator without the notch.

**Animations mentioned in release notes:**

- Answers **reveal word by word**, and spoken replies light up word by word in the notch
- An **adaptive progress ring** that reflects the active transcription stage while dictation processes
- A **thinking indicator** while the agent works
- **Stacked previews** that animate and expand based on their contents
- Smooth handoff between reminder card, side notch, and active conversation
- Reminder cards that update in place and collapse into an expandable notification stack
